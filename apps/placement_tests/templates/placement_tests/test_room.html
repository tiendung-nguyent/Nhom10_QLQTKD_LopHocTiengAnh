ĐỀ BÀI TASK 2

{% if goal_type == 'GIAO_TIEP' %} In modern workplaces, many companies have transitioned to flexible hybrid remote working arrangements. Do you think the benefits of remote work outweigh the disadvantages for team collaboration and corporate productivity? Support your opinion with reasons and business examples. Write between 180 and 220 words. {% elif goal_type == 'DAU_RA_DH' %} Some educators believe that tertiary institutions should focus solely on technical and career skills, while others argue that general education and soft skills are equally vital. Discuss both views and give your opinion. Write between 180 and 220 words. {% elif goal_type == 'MAT_GOC' %} English has become the global language for work, travel, and international communication. Why is learning English important for your personal growth and future opportunities? Give reasons and examples from your daily life. Write between 180 and 220 words. {% else %} In recent years, remote learning and hybrid working have become widespread across many countries. Gợi ý bố cục 4 đoạn:
1. Mở bài: Paraphrase vấn đề & Nêu quan điểm cá nhân.
2. Thân bài 1: Luận điểm thứ nhất + Ví dụ minh họa.
3. Thân bài 2: Luận điểm đối nghịch hoặc bổ trợ + Phân tích.
4. Kết luận: Tóm tắt quan điểm chung (1-2 câu).
